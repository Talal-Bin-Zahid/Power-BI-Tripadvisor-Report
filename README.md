# 🏨 TripAdvisor Report

### User Demographics, Hotel Performance & Seasonal Trends Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes TripAdvisor user and hotel data to uncover actionable insights into **traveler demographics**, **hotel performance**, **capacity distribution**, and **seasonal travel behavior**.

The report is designed to support **hospitality stakeholders**, **hotel managers**, and **travel analysts** in understanding customer segments, identifying top-performing hotels, and optimizing business strategies based on demand patterns.

## 📈 Visualizations Used

* **Bar Charts:** User distribution by continent, traveler type, and hotel capacity
* **Donut Charts:** Hotel ratings, seasonal stays, and hotel performance
* **Column Charts:** Hotel star rating distribution

All visuals are **interactive**, enabling dynamic filtering and quick comparison of trends.

## 📌 Conclusions & Business Value

* **Couples** are the dominant traveler segment, presenting opportunities for romantic and leisure-focused packages.
* **North America** is the primary market, making it a key focus for marketing and promotions.
* **Luxury and high-capacity hotels** consistently achieve higher ratings and accommodate the majority of demand.
* **Even seasonal distribution** supports stable pricing strategies and year-round promotional campaigns.
